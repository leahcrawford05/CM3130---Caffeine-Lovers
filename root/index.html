Welcome to the rebels’ corner

Lost & Ground started at a little crêpe hut on Aberdeen beach. Every day we watched bags of used coffee go in the bin. So we turned that waste into something good: a natural body scrub that leaves your skin soft and keeps plastic out of the sea.

Get to know us

About us

We make simple body scrubs from coffee that would otherwise end up in the bin.

It all began with Sarah’s crêpe hut by the sea in Aberdeen. Today we work with ReviveEco, who collect used coffee from cafés and big coffee chains around the UK. We turn it into a scrub you’ll actually want to use, in a jar you can keep.
