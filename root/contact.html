Good to know

Frequently asked

When are you launching?

We’re aiming for early 2027. Sign up to our newsletter and you’ll be the first to know.

Is the scrub safe for sensitive skin?

Our scrub is made with natural ingredients, but everyone’s skin is different. Try a little on your arm first. Please don’t use it on your face, on broken skin or after too much sun.

How is my order packed?

In cardboard, with no plastic fillers. Everything is recyclable or reusable.

Do you ship internationally?

For now we ship across the UK. We hope to ship to Europe soon. Sign up to our newsletter to hear about it first.

Where does your coffee come from?

From our partner ReviveEco. They collect used coffee from cafés in Glasgow and Edinburgh and from one of the UK’s biggest coffee chains, so every batch is consistent.

What can I do with the empty jar?

Reuse it! It’s perfect for spices, cotton pads, plants or your next refill.
